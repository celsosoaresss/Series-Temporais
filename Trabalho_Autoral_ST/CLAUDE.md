## Project

Course assignment (Séries Temporais, in Portuguese — keep code/docs in Portuguese): **Self-Supervised Learning for Time Series** — a BERT-style masked-reconstruction Transformer that imputes missing values in time series, pretrained on the UCI ElectricityLoadDiagrams20112014 dataset and designed to be re-evaluated on other datasets. Not a git repository.

## Pipeline

Run the notebooks in order; each consumes the previous one's artifacts:

1. `01_preprocessamento.ipynb` — loads `LD2011_2014.txt`, resamples 15min→1h, keeps 2012–2014, selects 100 clients (seed 42), temporal train/val/test split (2012–13 / 2014-S1 / 2014-S2), per-series z-score with **train-only stats**, windows of 168h → writes `dados_processados/` (janelas.npz, normalizador.json, config_dados.json)
2. `02_treinamento.ipynb` — trains `BERTImputador` (masked MSE, point+block masking, early stopping) → writes `resultados/melhor_modelo.pt`, `historico.json`
3. `03_avaliacao.ipynb` — fixed-seed evaluation masks (seed 123), 6 scenarios (pontual/bloco × 10/25/50%), baselines comparison → `resultados/metricas_teste.{csv,json}`; last section is the template for applying the method to a new dataset
4. `04_saits_fft.ipynb` — SAITS adaptation (Du et al. 2023) with a spectral [FREQ] token (rFFT of the visible window) replacing SAITS' 2nd DMSA block; trains two variants under notebook-02's budget — ablation `usar_fft=False` → `resultados/saits_base/`, full model → `resultados/saits_fft/` — then compares both + BERT + baselines under notebook-03's exact protocol → `resultados/metricas_saits.{csv,json}`. Never overwrites the BERT artifacts. **Finding (2026-07-04, seed 42):** the no-FFT ablation (DMSA + joint MIT+ORT loss, single block) beats BERT on MAE in all 6 scenarios (−0.1…−3.1%); the spectral token is used by the model (zeroing it costs +7–8% MAE on blocks — see `resultados/saits_fft/diagnostico_token.json`) but is redundant with what attention extracts from context, netting slightly negative. The [FREQ] token is a documented negative-result ablation.
5. `05_refinamento.ipynb` — **the work's main contribution: SAITS-2P**, iterative refinement with shared weights (`SAITSFFT(n_passadas=2, usar_fft=False)`): pass 1 imputes, gaps are filled with those imputations (SAITS eq. 13) and the same encoder refines — SAITS' 2nd-block benefit via iteration, zero new parameters (identical 150,337). Trains → `resultados/saits_refino/`, evaluates all 4 models + baselines → `resultados/metricas_refino.{csv,json}`; the 1-pass ablation is the existing `saits_base` checkpoint. **Finding (2026-07-04, seed 42):** SAITS-2P beats BERT on MAE *and* RMSE in all 6 scenarios (MAE −0.5…−4.6%) and the 1-pass variant on 5/6, with gains concentrated in the hard scenarios; per-pass diagnostic (`resultados/saits_refino/diagnostico_passadas.json`) confirms pass 2 improves pass 1 exactly there (bloco 50% −2.3%, pontual 50% −3.6%). `resultados/comparacao_modelos.csv` is the canonical 4-model side-by-side (métrica × cenário, deltas vs BERT), seed 42.
6. `06_sementes.ipynb` — multi-seed robustness: retrains the 4 models with seeds 43/44 (seed 42 reuses existing checkpoints; idempotent loop skips done checkpoints) → `resultados/sementes/{bert,saits1p,saits2p,saitsfft}_s{43,44}/`, evaluates 12 models under the fixed protocol → `resultados/metricas_sementes.{csv,json}`, aggregates → `resultados/comparacao_sementes.csv` (mean±std) and `resultados/deltas_pareados_sementes.csv` (seed-paired deltas). **Finding (2026-07-05, seeds 42–44):** SAITS-2P beats BERT in 18/18 paired MAE and 18/18 RMSE comparisons (worst-seed delta still negative everywhere); SAITS 1P also 18/18; 2P beats 1P consistently only in the hard scenarios (pontual/bloco 50%: 3/3 each). SAITS-FFT is on-average slightly better than BERT across seeds (seed 42 was unfavorable) but has the highest variance and is worse than the no-FFT variants in all 6 scenario means — the negative-result conclusion stands. Use these two CSVs (not the seed-42 ones) for the 40-epoch report numbers.
7. `07_fft_segmentada.ipynb` — segmented-FFT (Welch) experiment under a **new budget: 300 epochs / patience 30** (numbers NOT comparable to notebooks 03–06). 3×2 factorial {sem FFT, FFT padrão, FFT segmentada (6×48h segments, 50% overlap, per-bin averaging → 75 dims vs 255)} × {BERT family (full attention, [MASK], MIT — new `BERTFFT` class), SAITS family (DMSA, MIT+ORT)}, all retrained from scratch, seed 42 → `resultados/fft_segmentada/{bert,bert_fftp,bert_ffts,saits,saits_fftp,saits_ffts}/`, `resultados/metricas_fft_seg.{csv,json}`, `comparacao_fft_seg.csv`, `deltas_fft_seg.csv`. **Finding (2026-07-06, seed 42):** long training dominates (all models −8…−12% MAE vs the 40-epoch budget); with long training the FFT token NOW helps BERT (−1.6…−3.6% in all scenarios) but still adds nothing to SAITS — spectral input and ORT are two non-additive routes to the same information; segmented-FFT ties standard-FFT on accuracy with 3× fewer spectral dims (efficiency, not accuracy, is its contribution); five models tie at the top (mean MAE 0.1434–0.1438), only plain BERT lags (0.1472). SAITS-2P was not run at this budget — natural next step.

8. `08_adversarial.ipynb` — adversarial training (GAIN-style, Yoon et al. 2018, no hint vector) on the best long-budget BERT: generator = `BERTFFT` segmentada (time + FFT inputs), discriminator = `adversarial.DiscriminadorMascara` (2-layer Transformer + [FREQ] token, per-position BCE vs the mask), G loss = masked MSE + 0.1·adversarial, early stopping on G's val masked-MSE. Trains ONLY the new model (~2h; notebook-07 numbers loaded from `metricas_fft_seg.csv`, never re-run) → `resultados/adversarial/`, `resultados/metricas_adv.{csv,json}`, `comparacao_adv.csv`. **Finding (2026-07-06, seed 42):** clean negative result — adversarial is worse than its non-adversarial twin in all 6 scenarios (MAE +2.7…+4.2%, RMSE +1.8…+3.3%), landing ≈ plain BERT; training was stable (D loss ~0.54, no divergence), so the loss reflects the structural conflict between distributional realism and pointwise-error metrics. Closes the user's adversarial-contribution idea with a documented negative result.

9. `09_generalizacao.ipynb` — generalization to the SAITS paper's other datasets under the PAPER's protocol (their splits — test is the FIRST period —, sample lengths 24/24/48, train-only standardization, 10% of observed values eliminated as ground truth, early stop on val MAE/patience 30): trains BERT+FFT segmentada (n_segmentos=3) from scratch per dataset → `resultados/generalizacao/{airq,ett,physionet}/`, `resultados/metricas_generalizacao.{csv,json}`, `comparacao_generalizacao.csv` (our model + our Median/Last calibration vs Table-2 numbers). Datasets live in `dados_externos/` (Air-Quality PRSA 12 CSVs, ETTm1.csv, PhysioNet set-a/b/c tarballs + `physionet_grade.npz` cache; counts match the paper's Table 1 exactly). **Finding (2026-07-06, seed 42):** Air-Quality MAE 0.136 ≈ published SAITS 0.137 (beats BRITS/Transformer/SAITS-base); ETT 0.117 between Transformer (0.114) and SAITS-base (0.121), behind full SAITS (0.092); PhysioNet 0.319 vs 0.186–0.256 — the channel-independent design can't use cross-variable clinical correlations at 80% native missingness (documented structural limit). Calibration: our Median matches the paper's ±10%; the paper's "Last" is far worse than true LOCF (implementation difference) — comparisons with published rows are approximate.

All reusable logic lives in `src/` (notebooks only orchestrate): `dados.py` (load/split/normalize/window — generic over any datetime-indexed DataFrame), `mascaramento.py` (point + geometric block masking, vectorized), `modelo.py` (BERTImputador: bidirectional Transformer encoder, learnable [MASK] token, sinusoidal PE computed dynamically so any window length works), `saits_fft.py` (SAITSFFT: single diagonally-masked self-attention block + spectral token; `carregar_saits`), `treino.py` (seeded training loop; `fn_perda` selects the objective — `perda_mit_ort` is SAITS' joint loss in MSE form, validation/early stopping always uses masked-only MSE so histories stay comparable), `avaliacao.py` (reproducible evaluation protocol; `avaliar_metodos` accepts one model or a `{name: model}` dict), `adversarial.py` (GAIN-style loop: `DiscriminadorMascara` + `treinar_adversarial`; generator checkpoint stays loadable by `carregar_bert_fft`), `generalizacao.py` (SAITS-paper protocol on external datasets: loaders for Air-Quality/ETT/PhysioNet, native-missingness support — artificial masks drawn only over observed positions, loss on artificial only —, `treinar_faltantes`, paper-style MAE/RMSE/MRE, Median/Last calibration baselines), `baselines.py`, `estilo.py` (matplotlib palette; model colors — BERT=azul, SAITS ablation=verde_agua, SAITS-FFT=violeta, linear interp=amarelo — keep them stable across notebooks).

Reproducibility is a hard requirement: everything seeded, configs saved alongside artifacts, evaluation masks deterministic. Loss/metrics are always computed only on masked positions.
